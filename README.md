# 🎓 SARASWATI

> **Your Notes. Your Knowledge. Your AI.**

An AI-powered academic workspace that helps students organize their study materials and receive accurate, context-aware answers generated exclusively from their own uploaded documents.

# 📖 Overview

Students often manage multiple PDFs, notes, books, and assignments across different platforms. Finding the right information during revision is time-consuming and inefficient.

SARASWATI transforms these scattered study materials into an intelligent, searchable knowledge base that students can interact with using natural language.

Unlike conventional AI assistants, SARASWATI generates responses exclusively from the student's uploaded documents, ensuring personalized, reliable, and context-aware learning.

---

# ✨ Features

- 📂 Organize study materials by subject
- 📄 Upload and manage PDF documents
- 🤖 AI-powered question answering
- 🎯 Answers based only on uploaded documents
- 📚 Source-aware responses for verification
- 🔍 Intelligent document search
- 🔒 Private workspace for every user
- 💬 Interactive AI learning experience

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Students spend significant time searching through scattered notes, textbooks, and PDFs before exams. Generic AI assistants frequently provide answers based on external knowledge, which may not match a student's curriculum or personal study material.

SARASWATI solves this by creating a personalized AI assistant that understands and answers questions using only the student's own academic resources.

---

# 💡 Solution

SARASWATI enables students to upload their study materials into a secure workspace where they become easily searchable. Students can ask questions in natural language and receive answers grounded in their own documents, making learning faster, more reliable, and personalized.

---

# 🚀 How It Works

1. Create your workspace.
2. Upload notes and PDFs.
3. The platform understands and organizes your study materials.
4. Ask questions in natural language.
5. Receive accurate answers with references from your uploaded documents.
